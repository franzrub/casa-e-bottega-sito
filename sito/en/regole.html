The rules

A peaceful stay for everyone.

🕐
Check-in and Check-out

Check-in is available from 15:00 onwards. Check-out must take place by 11:30.

👥
Guests

The number of guests cannot exceed the number indicated during booking. External visitors are not allowed without prior communication.

🤫
Quiet and Respect for Neighbors

Please keep a moderate tone of voice after 23:00 and before 8:00, respecting other guests and residents.

🚬
Smoking

Smoking is prohibited inside apartments. Smoking is allowed only in designated outdoor spaces.

🐾
Pets

Pets are not allowed, except by written agreement with management.

❌
Cancellation

Free cancellation up to 15 days before check-in. Between 14 and 7 days: 50% refund. Under 7 days: no refund.
